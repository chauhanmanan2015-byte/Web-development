Explore the breathtaking diversity of marine life that inhabits our planet's vast oceans.

Featured Marine Species

Coral Reef

Clownfish

Small, vibrant orange fish recognized by their striking white bars outlined in black. Famous for their symbiotic relationship with sea anemones.

Habitat: Shallow warm waters

Fun Fact: All clownfish are born male!

Open Ocean

Blue Whale

The largest animal known to have ever lived on Earth, reaching lengths of up to 100 feet and weighing as much as 200 tonnes.

Habitat: All oceans except Arctic

Fun Fact: Heart size of a small car!

Open Ocean

Great White Shark

A famously powerful apex predator known for its size, torpedo-shaped body, and incredible sensory tracking abilities.

Habitat: Coastal surface waters

Fun Fact: Can detect a drop of blood from miles away.

Deep Sea

Anglerfish

Famous for the characteristic luminescent lure protruding from their heads, used to attract prey in pitch-black waters.

Habitat: Bathypelagic deep zone

Fun Fact: Males permanently fuse to females.

Coral Reef

Green Sea Turtle

Large, air-breathing marine reptiles named for the greenish color of their fat and cartilage, which eat seagrasses and algae.

Habitat: Tropical coastal areas

Fun Fact: Can hold breath for 5 hours.

Deep Sea

Giant Pacific Octopus

A remarkably intelligent cephalopod known for its capacity to camouflage, solve complex puzzles, and contort its body.

Habitat: Cold, temperate waters

Fun Fact: Three hearts and blue blood!
